# Disclaimer

This repository contains a fictional cybersecurity training exercise for learning SOC investigation, evidence correlation, incident reporting, and handover.

All organisations, people, domains, hostnames, and IP addresses are fictional or reserved for documentation and training. Do not scan, contact, visit, or otherwise interact with the example infrastructure.

Findings are limited to the evidence summaries supplied for the exercise. They are not a real-world forensic conclusion, legal determination, or breach-notification decision. Missing evidence is identified as missing; it is not inferred or fabricated.
